# Interview guide — UK DJ / event operator (O1)

**Purpose:** Recent behaviour and real documents — not enthusiasm as purchase intent.  
**Label all notes:** Verified fact (observed) · Reasoned inference · Working assumption · Unresolved question.  
**Forbid:** Invented quotations, invented interviews, treating “sounds cool” as WTP.

## Opening

- Consent to notes; no recording without explicit OK.
- Clarify: research for a local-first automix/radio tool; not selling in this call unless they ask.

## Recent behaviour

1. Walk me through your **last** paid or practice set from library → decks/software → end.
2. What tools did you actually open? (Rekordbox, Serato, djay, VirtualDJ, other)
3. How do you choose the **next** track today? How long does that take mid-set?
4. Have you used any **Automix / AI mix** feature in the last 90 days? What happened?
5. Do you ever need music to **keep going without you** (warm-up, changeover, background)?

## Pain and workarounds

6. What broke last time (key clash, energy cliff, silence, wrong vibe, licensing worry)?
7. Show (screen share or export) a recent playlist / Rekordbox XML / folder layout if willing.
8. What have you already paid for related to this job (software, stems, charts)? **GBP, date if known.**

## Alternatives

9. What would you do if Automix disappeared tomorrow?
10. Which competitor is “good enough”? Where does it fail your job?

## Willingness to pay (not a close)

11. If a **paid concierge pilot** prepared continuous radio from **your** local libraries for a fixed window, what would need to be true to try it?
12. Fee reaction: founder may state a **GBP ex-VAT** band from `finance/pricing.md` (hypothesis only); else leave blank — do not invent prices.
13. Who approves spend if you wanted new software?

## Close

14. Who else has this problem? Referral OK?
15. Negative finding: what would make this irrelevant?
16. Permission to follow up once a concrete pilot offer exists?

## After

- Update recruitment tracker, synthesis template, negative-findings log, assumption register.
- Never commit customer audio, playlists with personal data, or unredacted exports.
