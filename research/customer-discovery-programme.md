## Flow

1. **Recruit** — recruitment tracker / CRM named accounts (ICP L/U/R/N)
2. **Interview / observe** — interview guide + observation checklist
3. **Synthesise / log negatives** — interview synthesis + negative-findings log
4. **Scorecard Actuals** — update only from real evidence paths
5. **Pilot / commercial** — pilot brief (fee/waiver founder-owned) + CRM stages
6. **Continuation** — Customer / Lost; refresh retention rows only from evidence

## Rules

- Currency: **GBP ex-VAT** unless stated.
- Blank Actuals stay blank until founder logs real discovery.
- No bulk outreach before message and offer validation.
- Enthusiasm ≠ purchase intent.
- No implied partnership, endorsement, certification, or compliance without evidence.
