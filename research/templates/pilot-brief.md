# Paid concierge pilot brief — AutoDJ (O1)

**Status:** Template. Fee/waiver blank until founder sets them.  
**Principle:** Prefer paid concierge before building new software or cloud SaaS.

## Fixed scope

- Customer provides **user-owned** local library roots (paths they control).
- Founder/agent configures AutoDJ Radio libraries, Feature DB index, continuous radio and/or timed set (30–90 min) + vibe.
- Deliver: running radio/set workflow + MixReport QA review of at least one rendered or live-handoff session.
- **Out of scope:** stems, streaming OAuth, open-web sourcing, custom DSP features, multi-tenant cloud, legal music licensing.

## Responsibilities

| Party | Responsibility |
|-------|----------------|
| Customer | Lawful ownership/licence of audio; baseline time estimate for current workflow; Stop authority; feedback listens |
| Founder | Setup; scope control; data-handling boundary; continuation decision |

## Limits

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Volume limit | One library cohort / one event window (define per pilot) |
| Duration | ___ days/weeks (founder fills) |
| Fee (GBP ex-VAT) | ___ or **documented waiver** |
| VAT treatment | Ex-VAT unless stated |
| Baseline measurement | Minutes to prepare continuous/timed set today |
| Success criteria | Agreed in writing before start (e.g. radio runs without babysitting for N minutes; MixReport gates reviewed) |
| Data-handling | On-device/local; no commit of customer audio; no upload path in v1 product |
| Continuation decision point | End of duration → Customer / Lost on tracker |

## Measure

- Time saved (baseline vs pilot)
- Errors prevented or caught (MixReport / listen notes)
- Rework avoided
- Human review effort
- Support effort (founder hours)
- Repeat usage
- Willingness to continue paying

## Prohibit

Unsupported claims about sales uplift, safety, compliance, incidents, or ROI.  
AI/Softmax may assist transitions; **only the customer** approves consequential public performance use of their library.
