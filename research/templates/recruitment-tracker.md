|------|-----------|
| L | Owns/controls local music library |
| U | UK / England-reachable for outreach |
| R | Mobile/club DJ or small event operator (not anti-persona-only) |
| N | Not primarily enterprise radio / live dual-deck / cloud-sync-or-stems-only buyer |

Pass discovery recruit if L+U+R and not failing N. Else DQ → negative-findings.

## Rules

- No bulk outreach before message/offer validation.
- No implied partnership, endorsement, certification, or compliance.
- GBP fees ex-VAT when discussed; do not invent prices or commitments.
- Agents may refine this template; only the founder fills named rows from real outreach.
