# Interview guide

Based on founder master prompt §8. Duration target: 30–45 minutes.

## Opening

- Thank them; no pitch first.
- “We’re researching how people build mixes from their own libraries—not selling yet unless a pilot fits.”

## Core questions (recent behaviour)

1. **Most recent mix:** When did you last need a continuous mix from your library? Walk me through that session step by step.
2. **Artefacts:** Can you show the playlist, prep notes, or export you used? (Screen share / description only—no confidential commits.)
3. **Frequency & owner:** How often does this job happen? Who is accountable for the output?
4. **Consequence:** What happens if the mix is late or low quality?
5. **Tools today:** What software? Where does it fail for **this** job (not general gripes)?
6. **Approval:** If you wanted new software, who approves spend?
7. **Price test:** “For a 2-week pilot—one library, one deliverable mix, MixReport review—we’d charge **GBP X ex VAT**. Too high, fair, or bargain?” (Use number from `finance/pricing.md`.)

## Close

- Ask for referrals to similar operators.
- Ask permission to follow up after pilot offer is concrete.
- Log refusals in `negative-findings.md`.

## Do not

- Treat excitement as LOI.
- Record without consent.
- Commit to features not in charter.
