## Rules

- No bulk outreach before message and offer validation.
- No implied partnership, endorsement, certification, or compliance without evidence.
- Currency for any fee discussion: **GBP ex-VAT** unless stated; do not invent prices or commitments.
- Status Actuals stay blank until founder logs a real touch.
- From **Pilot offered** onward, use [`pilot-brief.md`](pilot-brief.md); fee/waiver remain founder-owned.
- Agents may refine this template; only the founder fills named rows from real outreach.
