# Interview guide — UK mobile/club DJ & small event operator (O1)

**Duration:** 30–45 minutes  
**ICP:** UK (England-first practical) mobile/club DJs and small event operators who own local music libraries  
**Opportunity:** O1 — continuous private radio + timed sets (see `Docs/project-charter.md`, `research/opportunity-portfolio.md`)  
**Purpose:** Recent behaviour and real documents — **not** enthusiasm as purchase intent  

**Label all notes:** Verified fact (observed) · Reasoned inference · Working assumption · Unresolved question  

**Forbid:** Invented quotations, invented interviews, treating “sounds cool” as WTP, inventing GBP fees, committing customer audio or unredacted playlists.

---

## Screening (2–3 min)

Stop or re-route (log in negative-findings) if clearly anti-persona.

| # | Screen | Pass if |
|---|--------|---------|
| S1 | Do you own / control a **local** music library (WAV/FLAC/folders or Rekordbox collection on disk)? | Yes |
| S2 | In the last 90 days, did you prepare a paid gig, practice set, or timed/continuous play from that library? | Yes |
| S3 | Is your main need enterprise radio procurement, live dual-deck residency hardware, or cloud library sync / stems-out-of-box? | **No** (those are anti-persona for now) |

If S1/S2 fail → thank them; optional referral ask; log DQ. If S3 is their only job → DQ to negative-findings (anti-persona).

---

## Opening

- Consent to notes; no recording without explicit OK.
- Clarify: research for a **local-first** automix / continuous-radio tool; not selling in this call unless they ask.
- Confirm geography/reachability for follow-up (UK / England-first — not a hard geo lock).

---

## Recent behaviour (continuous / timed leave-alone job)

1. Walk me through your **last** paid or practice set: library → prep software → decks/playback → end.
2. What tools did you actually open? (Rekordbox, Serato, djay, VirtualDJ, other)
3. How do you choose the **next** track today? How long does that take mid-set or mid-playlist?
4. Have you used any **Automix / AI mix** feature in the last 90 days? What happened?
5. Do you ever need music to **keep going without you** (warm-up, changeover, bar background, timed set while you step away)?
6. If yes: how long should it run (e.g. 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 min or until stop)? Who starts/stops it?
7. What broke last time you tried leave-alone or long automix (silence, energy cliff, key clash, wrong vibe, licensing worry)?

---

## Artefacts & workarounds

8. Show (screen share or describe) a recent playlist / Rekordbox XML / folder layout if willing — **do not** accept files into git.
9. What have you already paid for related to this job (software, stems, charts)? **GBP**, date if known — label Verified fact only if they state it.

---

## Alternatives & quality evidence

10. What would you do if Automix disappeared tomorrow?
11. Which competitor is “good enough” for continuous/timed play? Where does it fail **this** job?
12. Do you ever need **evidence** that a mix/handoff was OK (loudness, bad joins, something you could show a client or yourself later)? What do you use today?  
    *(Probe only — do not claim MixReport as certified listening quality.)*

---

## Trust, approval, willingness to pay (not a close)

13. How do you feel about pointing software at your **local** libraries with **no upload**? Any deal-breakers?
14. If you wanted new software, who approves spend?
15. If a **paid concierge pilot** set up continuous radio / timed sets from **your** local libraries for a fixed window, what would need to be true to try it?
16. Fee reaction: founder may state a **GBP ex-VAT** band from `finance/pricing.md` (hypothesis only); else leave blank — **do not invent prices**.

---

## Close

17. Who else has this problem? Referral OK?
18. Negative finding: what would make this irrelevant?
19. Permission to follow up once a concrete pilot offer exists?
